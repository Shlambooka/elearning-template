What is a feedback loop?

A feedback loop is a process where the output of a system circles back to influence its own input. In other words: the system reacts to its own results.

Feedback loops are everywhere — in biology, economics, engineering, and even in how you learn. They come in two flavours:

Quick intuition

Negative feedback resists change. Positive feedback amplifies change. The words describe the direction of the response, not whether the outcome is good or bad.
